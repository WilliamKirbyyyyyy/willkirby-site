Investigation · February 2026

What the app doesn't check.

On 4–5 February 2026 I recorded twelve listings on the Temu UK app for products that are age-restricted or banned from sale in the UK. Each could be added to the basket with no age check shown. I wrote it up with the law each listing engages and submitted it to Trading Standards.

  • 12 screenshots
  • Legislation mapped
  • Submitted to Trading Standards

01 — What I found

Six patterns, one gap.

  • 01Disposable vapes on sale, promoted on the homepage, though selling them has been unlawful UK-wide since 1 June 2025.
  • 02Vape hardware sold as a “power tool”, described as ideal for “indoor and outdoor repairs”: wording an age filter wouldn’t catch.
  • 03Sparklers listed under “Makeup”. Supplying these fireworks to under-18s is prohibited.
  • 04Bladed and pointed articles, which can’t be sold to under-18s, with no age step shown.
  • 05Tattoo inks with no visible compliance labelling in the listing.
  • 06Poisons and sharps in unrelated categories: weedkiller under Pet Food, rat poison under Insect Repellers, syringes under Kitchen Utensils.

The point about capability

Temu already blurs sexual-wellness listings behind an “Adults only” overlay, so the platform can gate a category. It didn’t appear to apply anything similar to vapes, fireworks, blades, pesticides or medical sharps.

04 — The report

What I asked for, and what I didn’t claim.

What I asked Trading Standards to do

  1. Log the listings for intelligence purposes.
  2. Assess the disposable-vape listings first, since that product is banned outright rather than sold without an age check.
  3. Consider whether filing vape hardware under “Power Tools” and fireworks under “Makeup” points to a systematic classification failure rather than isolated seller error.

Limits, stated plainly

The screenshots come from one browsing session, in the UK app only. I didn’t buy anything, so I make no claim about checks at checkout or delivery. A listing being available isn’t evidence that an underage sale happened, and the sales figures are Temu’s own.
