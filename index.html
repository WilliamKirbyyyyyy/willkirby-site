02 — Investigation

What the app doesn't check.

  • Report
  • Feb 2026
  • Trading Standards

In February 2026 I recorded twelve listings on the Temu UK app for products that are age-restricted or banned from sale in the UK. Each could be added to the basket with no age check shown. I wrote it up with the law each listing engages and submitted it to Trading Standards.

  • 01Disposable vapes on the homepage, though their sale has been banned UK-wide since June 2025.
  • 02Vape hardware filed under “Power Tools” and sparklers under “Makeup”: categories where an age filter wouldn’t look.
  • 03Weedkiller under “Pet Food”, rat poison under “Insect Repellers”, syringes under “Kitchen Utensils”.
Read the full investigation, with all 12 screenshots

12 dated screenshots · the relevant law mapped for each listing · limits stated plainly: one session, no purchase made, so no claim about checkout.
