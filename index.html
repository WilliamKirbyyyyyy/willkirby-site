RIVET

A native Windows system manager.

Windows · .NET 10 · WPF · Live

[01.1] What it does

A native Windows system manager. Live hardware stats, signed-process verification, startup impact ratings, plain-English tweaks, and Ask RIVET, a local AI that answers questions about your PC without sending anything off it. Version 3.7 adds Spark, an assistant you can call up over any app with one shortcut, answers that show the measurements behind them, before-and-after proof for every fix, and a first-run tour, and it runs on about a tenth of the CPU 3.6 needed. That builds on 3.6’s ten languages and four themes and 3.5’s Guard & Restore.

02 — Investigation

What the app doesn’t check.

[02.1] Summary

In February 2026 I recorded twelve listings on the Temu UK app for products that are age-restricted or banned from sale in the UK. Each could be added to the basket with no age check shown. I wrote it up with the law each listing engages and submitted it to Trading Standards.

  1. E1Disposable vapes on the homepage, though their sale has been banned UK-wide since June 2025.
  2. E2 · E3Vape hardware filed under “Power Tools” and sparklers under “Makeup”: categories where an age filter wouldn’t look.
  3. E5 · E7 · E8Weedkiller under “Pet Food”, rat poison under “Insect Repellers”, syringes under “Kitchen Utensils”.
