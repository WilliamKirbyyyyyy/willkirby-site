[04.1] Profile

I’m a student and indie developer, and the founder of Kirby Systems. I’ve been building software for years, and RIVET is the first thing I’ve shipped properly: it started as a PowerShell script and is now a native Windows app on the Microsoft Store.

I’m at Blundell’s School, where I’m an Academic Scholar and a DT Scholar. I’m aiming at machine learning and business. I’m learning ML in public through EPOCH, a daily video series that runs until RIVET hits 1,000 downloads, and I’m preparing for the UK Olympiad in AI.

I learn by shipping. Alongside the code there are microcontrollers, CAD and 3D printing.

[04.2] Music

Away from the screen.

I sing in four choirs at Blundell’s: the Full Choir, Chamber Choir, Boys’ Close Harmony and Cantorum. I also play in the Senior Orchestra and the Junior Instrumental Ensemble.

Highlights so far: concerts at Exeter Cathedral and Bath Abbey, a tour to Vienna singing at St Stephen’s Cathedral (the Stephansdom) and St Peter’s Church (the Peterskirche), and this term’s Halloween concert, with the Lacrimosa and the Dies Irae.

Grades so far: ABRSM Grade 3 Flute with Merit and Trinity Grade 3 Classical Guitar. I was awarded a year of funded singing lessons and I’m working towards Grade 5 Singing, plus Grade 4 on flute and guitar.

[04.4] Making

Hardware, too.

I design parts in CAD and print them, and build with microcontrollers like the ESP32 and RP2040. Right now I’m reprinting a DIY VR headset, designing a Voronoi watch for GCSE 3D Design, and planning a free-flight high-altitude balloon to around 29 km for a CREST Silver Award.

[04.5] Build sheet

The headset, layer by layer.

The new shell for my DIY VR headset, modelled in Fusion. Every dimension is a parameter, so swapping the screen or lenses rebuilds the whole thing.

  1. 01Frame

    Holds the 2K screen a fixed 35 mm behind the lenses.

  2. 02Lens rings

    Two 38 mm lenses, centred 63 mm apart to match the wearer’s eyes.

  3. 03Back cover

    A shelf that keeps the motion sensor flat with its cables pointing down, so head tracking reads true, plus a cradle for the Pro Micro board.

  4. 04Face rim

    Curved to sit against the face.

  5. 05Visor

    One double-curved shell over all the electronics, so it looks like a headset rather than a cardboard box.

  6. 06Assembled

    Six printed parts, each sized to fit the print bed in one piece.
